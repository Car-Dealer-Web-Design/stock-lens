# Try Vehicle Advert Builder and share what would make it more useful

This free browser tool is for independent car dealers. Put the key vehicle facts, price and viewing details into an advert that is easy for a buyer to read.

[Open the tool](https://car-dealer-web-design.github.io/stock-lens/). No coding knowledge, installation or account is needed. Start with the fictional example, or fill in the labelled fields with your own information. A copyable vehicle advert and a publication check for your stock team.

Feedback about unclear questions, missing practical options or mobile usability is welcome here. Please use fictional information in public examples and keep customer or confidential details out of this discussion.

This creates advert copy from your information. It does not check vehicle history, stock availability or legal and finance disclosures.
